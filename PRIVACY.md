# Privacy Policy 🔒

**Effective Date:** July 28, 2026

At **QuickThought**, we believe your thoughts, ideas, and notes belong strictly to you.

## 1. Zero Data Collection
- **No Telemetry**: We do not collect, track, or transmit usage statistics or analytics.
- **No Cloud Uploads**: Your notes never leave your personal machine.
- **No User Accounts**: QuickThought requires no login, email address, or user account.

## 2. Local-First Data Storage
- All data is stored in your operating system's local storage engine (SQLite / IndexedDB).
- You have 100% control over your files. You can export your data as `.md` (Markdown) or `.json` backups at any time from the Settings menu.

## 3. Third-Party Services
- QuickThought uses **zero third-party tracking SDKs**, zero advertising scripts, and zero remote analytics.

## 4. Updates to Policy
Since QuickThought does not collect any data, this privacy policy remains straightforward and unwavering. Any updates will be reflected in this repository.
